01 — Problem

Egyptian graduates are
drowning, not diving.

The problem isn't a lack of opportunities — it's a lack of strategic direction. Most graduates operate on trial-and-error, lacking a structured system to transition from speculative applying to a data-driven career path.

🔀

Resource Fragmentation

Users spend more time navigating between LinkedIn, YouTube, Coursera, and Udemy than actually building skills. The paradox of choice creates paralysis.

🕳

The Hidden Skill Gap

Candidates face automated rejections without feedback. They can't tell if failure is a resume issue, a skill deficit, or a targeting problem. No clarity, no improvement.

Burnout Without Progress

Without a centralized system to visualize progress, graduates experience apathy. When effort doesn't correlate with outcomes, they stop investing entirely.

Market Disconnection

Academic curricula lag years behind Egyptian startup and corporate ecosystem demands. Theoretical knowledge meets zero practical runway.

04 — Solution

One app. Five
intelligent systems.

"Sanad audits what you know, maps where you need to go, and walks with you every day — so your next step is always clear, never overwhelming."

Rather than adding another content platform to the noise, Sanad acts as an intelligent layer on top of the user's career journey. It transforms a chaotic job search into a structured, measurable, 30-day mission — with AI-powered guidance that adapts to the user's skills, energy levels, and the Egyptian market.

🎯

Focus over Volume

One primary CTA per screen. No decision fatigue. One next step, always.

📊

Data with Direction

Every score is paired with an action. Readiness 68% → "Complete Flexbox to reach 70%."

🧠

Psychological Safety

Burnout detection runs silently in the background and intervenes before the user breaks.

Product Thinking — Usability

Users may struggle with X.
We solved it with Y.

🚫 Problem: Too many insights overwhelm the user
↓
✅ Solution: 1 Primary Action Rule

Every screen has exactly one primary CTA. Secondary information is visually demoted. No screen competes for attention.

🚫 Problem: Arabic RTL layout breaks visual flow
↓
✅ Solution: Bilingual identity system

Separate icon lockups for English and Arabic. RTL-aware component layout. سند wordmark designed to work independently.

🚫 Problem: Users don't know when they're "ready" to apply
↓
✅ Solution: Career Readiness Score

Quantified 0–100 readiness metric paired with a specific next action. Removes ambiguity: 68% → "Complete Flexbox to reach 70%."

🚫 Problem: Burnout is detected too late — user has already quit
↓
✅ Solution: Proactive background monitoring

Burnout meter runs silently. Triggers recovery mode before the user hits breaking point — not after they've already left.
